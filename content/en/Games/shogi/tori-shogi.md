Each side starts with a phoenix, a left and a right quail, a falcon, two cranes, two pheasants and eight swallows. Sente moves first. Checkmate the enemy phoenix while keeping yours safe. Little Golem draws a fourfold repetition.

Move one bird or drop a captured bird onto an empty square. A captured bird returns to its original form in hand. A swallow cannot drop on the last rank, give immediate mate by a drop, or become a third unpromoted swallow of its own side on the same file. A falcon or swallow must promote when its move starts or ends in the farthest two ranks. Dropping a bird into that zone leaves it unpromoted.

## Piece guide

Forward is **up** in every diagram. The dark tile is the starting square. A **dot** marks a reachable square, an **arrow** means any distance on a clear line, and a **diamond** marks a jump over intervening pieces. Slides stop at the first occupied square. You may capture an opposing piece there.

### Phoenix · 鵬

{{< shogi-move "king" "Phoenix · 鵬" >}}
Steps one square in any direction. The phoenix is the royal bird.

Does not promote.

### Left quail · 鶉

{{< shogi-move "leftQuail" "Left quail · 鶉" >}}
Slides straight forward or diagonally backward to its right. It can step one square diagonally backward to its left.

Left and right are from the moving player’s perspective. Does not promote.
{{< /shogi-move >}}

### Right quail · 鶉

{{< shogi-move "rightQuail" "Right quail · 鶉" >}}
Slides straight forward or diagonally backward to its left. It can step one square diagonally backward to its right.

The mirror image of the left quail. Does not promote.
{{< /shogi-move >}}

### Falcon · 鷹

{{< shogi-move "toriFalcon" "Falcon · 鷹" >}}
Steps one square in every direction except straight backward.

Promotes to the **eagle**.
{{< /shogi-move >}}

### Eagle · 鵰

{{< shogi-move "toriEagle" "Eagle · 鵰" >}}
Slides straight backward or diagonally forward. It can step straight forward or sideways; on either backward diagonal it can move one or two squares. The two-square diagonal move requires an empty intervening square.

A captured eagle returns to hand as a falcon.
{{< /shogi-move >}}

### Crane · 鶴

{{< shogi-move "crane" "Crane · 鶴" >}}
Steps one square straight forward, straight backward or along any diagonal.

Does not promote.
{{< /shogi-move >}}

### Pheasant · 雉

{{< shogi-move "pheasant" "Pheasant · 雉" >}}
Jumps exactly two squares straight forward, or steps one square diagonally backward.

The forward jump clears an occupied square. Does not promote.
{{< /shogi-move >}}

### Swallow · 燕

{{< shogi-move "pawn" "Swallow · 燕" >}}
Steps one square straight forward. It captures the same way it moves.

Promotes to the **goose**.
{{< /shogi-move >}}

### Goose · 鴈

{{< shogi-move "goose" "Goose · 鴈" >}}
Jumps exactly two squares diagonally forward or straight backward, even over an occupied square.

A captured goose returns to hand as a swallow.
{{< /shogi-move >}}
