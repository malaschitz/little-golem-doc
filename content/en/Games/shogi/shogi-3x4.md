Each player starts with a lion, a giraffe, an elephant and a chick. Sente moves first. Checkmate wins. You can also win by moving your lion safely onto the opponent’s back rank.

On your turn, move a piece or drop one from your hand onto an empty square. A captured hen becomes a chick in hand. A chick reaching the last rank becomes a hen; a chick may not drop on that rank. You may drop a chick on a file that already holds one of yours, or give immediate mate with a chick drop. Little Golem draws a fourfold repetition.

## Piece guide

Forward is **up** in every diagram. The dark tile is the starting square. A **dot** marks a reachable square, an **arrow** means any distance on a clear line, and a **diamond** marks a jump over intervening pieces. Slides stop at the first occupied square. You may capture an opposing piece there.

### Lion · 🦁

{{< shogi-move "king" "Lion · 🦁" >}}
Steps one square in any direction. Your lion must be safe when it reaches the far rank to win by campmate.

Does not promote.

### Giraffe · 🦒

{{< shogi-move "orthostep" "Giraffe · 🦒" >}}
Steps one square straight forward, backward or sideways.

Does not promote.
{{< /shogi-move >}}

### Elephant · 🐘

{{< shogi-move "diagstep" "Elephant · 🐘" >}}
Steps one square along any diagonal.

Does not promote.
{{< /shogi-move >}}

### Chick · 🐥

{{< shogi-move "pawn" "Chick · 🐥" >}}
Steps one square straight forward. It captures the same way it moves.

Promotes to a **hen** on the far rank.
{{< /shogi-move >}}

### Hen · 🐔

{{< shogi-move "gold" "Hen · 🐔" >}}
Steps one square straight forward, diagonally forward, sideways or straight backward.

The hen is a promoted chick. Capture returns it to hand as a chick.
{{< /shogi-move >}}
