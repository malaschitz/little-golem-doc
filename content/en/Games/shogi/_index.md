Choose your variant for its setup, promotion rules and illustrated piece guide. The royal piece is the king in standard Shogi, the phoenix in Tori Shogi and the lion in Animal Shogi. In Chu Shogi, a promoted drunk elephant becomes a second royal piece, and captured pieces leave play permanently.
