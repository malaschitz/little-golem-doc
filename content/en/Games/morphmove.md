## Original 6v6

White moves first. Each player starts with six pieces on a board of five files
and seven ranks. White starts with triangle, star, triangle on b1, c1, d1 and
diamond, star, diamond on b2, c2, d2. Black mirrors this on ranks 7 and 6.

Move one piece by one square. A triangle steps straight forward, toward the
opponent's home rank. A diamond steps diagonally. A star steps in any of the
eight directions. After moving, a triangle becomes a diamond, a diamond
becomes a star, and a star becomes a triangle.

Moving onto an opposing piece swaps the two pieces and advances both symbols.
The opponent cannot immediately reverse that swap. Your own pieces block you.

The centre cross is c3, b4, c4, d4 and c5. End your turn on at least three
of these squares and hold at least three through your opponent's reply to win.
A player with no legal move loses. A third occurrence of the same position
with the same player to move and the same swap rights draws the game.

[Designer’s illustrated rules](https://play.morphmove.com/instructions)

## 8v8

Play on the same 5 × 7 board. White's back rank, from a1 to e1, is
diamond, triangle, star, triangle, diamond. Put moons on b2 and d2
and a star on c2. Black mirrors this setup on ranks 7 and 6.

A moon moves one square diagonally forward, toward the opponent's home rank.
The cycle after moving or swapping is triangle → moon → diamond → star → triangle.
If your opponent holds three squares of the centre cross, your reply must
reduce that number below three. If no move can do so, your opponent wins.

[Play the designer's 8v8 edition](https://play.morphmove.com/)
