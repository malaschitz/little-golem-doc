## Playing a turn

Draw seven tiles. The first word crosses the centre of the 15×15 board. On later turns, add one or more tiles in one row or column, connecting to the existing position. Existing tiles may fill gaps. Every new word, including a word running across the main word, must belong to the edition chosen for the game. A blank represents the letter you choose and scores zero.

Place tiles from your rack, review the words and points, then choose **Send move**. **Cancel** returns the tiles. To exchange, open **Game actions → Exchange**, select one or more tiles and confirm. You need at least seven tiles in the bag and the exchange uses your turn. You can also pass. Open the bag to see how many tiles each player holds and how many remain to draw.

## Scoring and the end of play

Add the letter values in each newly formed word. Letter bonuses apply before word bonuses. Only tiles placed this turn activate bonus squares. A new tile shared by two words counts in both. Using seven rack tiles in one turn earns 50 extra points.

Play ends when a player uses their last tile with the bag empty, or after six consecutive individual turns scoring zero. Both players subtract the value of the tiles on their racks. A player who empties their rack also adds the opponent’s remaining tile value. The higher final score wins. Equal scores draw.

## Dictionary editions

The flag identifies the language. The two English editions have separate dictionaries. The tile distribution, points and accepted words depend on the edition. Arabic and Hebrew run right to left across the board. Japanese uses hiragana readings and may show kanji in the move history.
