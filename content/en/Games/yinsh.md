title: "YINSH"
weight: 380
description: "Ring movement, scoring rows and the Blitz subvariant."
---

White begins. Take turns placing a ring of your colour on any vacant
intersection until both players have placed five rings.

On your turn, leave a marker of your colour under one of your rings and slide
that ring along a line. You can cross empty points and jump one uninterrupted
group of markers of either colour. After a jump, land in the first empty space
and flip every marker you jumped. Another ring blocks passage.

Five consecutive markers of your colour in a straight line form a row. Return
those five markers to the common pool and take one of your own rings off the
board. When there are several possible rows, choose one and repeat while a
row remains. If your move makes rows for both players, resolve yours first.
Your opponent resolves theirs before sliding a ring.

In Standard, taking off your third ring wins immediately. In the Blitz
subvariant, the first row wins. If all 51 markers are on the board after
resolving rows, whoever has removed more rings wins. Equal totals give a draw,
including a game in which neither player has ever made a row.

[Illustrated rules by Kris Burm](https://www.gipf.com/yinsh/rules/rules.html)

If every ring belonging to the player to move is blocked, the reported
clarification from the designer is to pass. A move is compulsory as soon as
one is available. [Source for this clarification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YINSH#Movement)
