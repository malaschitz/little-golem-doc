Little Golem provides asynchronous board games, tournaments and community
communication. By using the service, players agree to the following operational
rules.

## Accounts

Use one account per person unless the administrator has approved a separate bot
or testing account. Keep account credentials private and provide a working email
address for recovery and service notifications.

## Fair play

Do not manipulate ratings, tournament results or another player's account.
Automation is allowed only where the published [bot rules](/bots/) permit it.
Game-specific rules and tournament conditions also apply.

## Messages and public content

Players are responsible for their forum posts, game chat, profile text and
private messages. Do not use Little Golem to harass other players, distribute
malicious content or publish another person's private information.

The administrator may remove content or restrict an account when needed to
protect players, the service or fair competition.

## Availability and changes

The service may be interrupted for maintenance, security work or technical
failures. Features and these terms may change as Little Golem evolves. Important
changes should be announced through the site.

Account and technical data are handled as described in the
[privacy policy](/privacy/). Questions can be sent through the
[contact page](/contact/).
