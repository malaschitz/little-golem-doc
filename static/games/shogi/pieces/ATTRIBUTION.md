# Chu Shogi international pieces

Artwork by CouchTomato87, distributed in [Lishogi’s Chu Shogi international set](https://github.com/WandererXII/lishogi/tree/268c10c7f11d64ad306090c397bb38319c8d8cd3/ui/%40build/pieces/assets/chushogi/intl). The international artwork is credited under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) in [Lishogi’s credits](https://github.com/WandererXII/lishogi/blob/268c10c7f11d64ad306090c397bb38319c8d8cd3/COPYING.md).

Little Golem rasterizes the upright pieces at 256 pixels high, names them by their original role and promotion state, and rotates them on the board.
